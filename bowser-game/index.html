Reach the castle before the 5 minute timer ends, survive the enemies, defeat Bowser, and carry Peach through three brutal levels.

WASD moves. Tap W twice to double jump. Press Space to punch. When your HP drops below 100, you unlock the AK-47, which deals 150 damage per second while Space is held. Saving Peach opens harder levels.
